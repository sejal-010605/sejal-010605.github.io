About

Building a foundation in structural engineering through numerical modelling, computational mechanics and research-oriented engineering.

I am a Civil and Infrastructure Engineering undergraduate at IIT Jodhpur, currently exploring structural behaviour through computational modelling and numerical simulation.

My work spans finite element analysis, structural stability, cold-formed steel, circular hollow sections, bolted connections and data-driven engineering.

I enjoy working at the boundary between physical understanding and computation — developing models, automating analyses and using data to investigate engineering behaviour.

Research

Current and recent research exploring imperfections, structural stability and computational approaches to engineering problems.

June 2026 — Present

Geometric Imperfection Modelling of CHS

Research Intern · NUS Singapore
Dr. Meng Xin

Developing mathematical descriptions of geometric imperfections in Circular Hollow Sections (CHS) and investigating their influence on structural response.

  • Developed three-dimensional imperfection models using controlled wave numbers, amplitude and phase.
  • Automated finite-element modelling and Static Riks analysis using Python.
  • Generated simulation datasets for investigating data-driven prediction of structural behaviour.
  • Extending the modelling framework towards additively manufactured materials.
ABAQUS Python Static Riks CHS Machine Learning
May 2025 — July 2025

Computational Modelling of Bolted Joints

Research Intern · IIT Bombay
Dr. Tushar Kanti Mandal

Short-term computational study contributing to a planned research project with ISRO, investigating the behaviour of bolted connections under static and dynamic loading.

  • Developed nonlinear contact models of bolted joints using ABAQUS.
  • Investigated fatigue and loosening behaviour under dynamic loading.
  • Automated simulation post-processing using Python.
