02 / RESEARCH
Questions I like to investigate.

My research interests are centred around computational structural mechanics and the use of numerical and data-driven approaches to understand structural behaviour.

01

Geometric Imperfections

Development of mathematical imperfection models for Circular Hollow Sections, investigating controlled wave numbers, amplitudes and phase parameters.

PYTHON · ABAQUS · NONLINEAR ANALYSIS
02

Cold-Formed Steel

Numerical investigation of edge-stiffened perforated cold-formed steel channel sections and their structural response under compression.

ABAQUS · CUFSM · STRUCTURAL BUCKLING
03

Bolted Connections

Finite element modelling of bolted joints under static and dynamic loading, with emphasis on contact behaviour, fatigue and loosening response.

03 / SELECTED PROJECTS
From equations to models.

A selection of computational, structural and data-driven engineering projects.

PROJECT 01

Geometric Imperfection Modelling of CHS

Developed a computational framework to generate controlled geometric imperfection shapes for Circular Hollow Sections by varying wave numbers, amplitudes and phase.

PYTHON · ABAQUS · RIKS ANALYSIS · COMPUTATIONAL MECHANICS
PROJECT 02

Edge-Stiffened Perforated CFS

Numerical investigation of perforated cold-formed steel channel sections, studying structural response and buckling behaviour using finite element analysis.

ABAQUS · CUFSM · BUCKLING ANALYSIS
PROJECT 03

Concrete Strength Prediction using ML

Developed a machine-learning workflow for predicting concrete compressive strength from mix proportions and age, including preprocessing, normalization and model evaluation.

PYTHON · TENSORFLOW · KERAS · MACHINE LEARNING
PROJECT 04

Design Cost Optimization

Explored computational approaches for structural design and cost optimization, connecting engineering constraints with algorithmic search and data-driven decision making.

PYTHON · OPTIMIZATION · STRUCTURAL DESIGN
PROJECT 05

Direct Stiffness Structural Analysis

Developed a Python-based Direct Stiffness Method solver with a graphical interface for analysis of 2D plane frames and validation against STAAD Pro.

PYTHON · NUMPY · STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS
PROJECT 06

5D BIM & Construction Planning

Developed a 10-storey Autodesk Revit BIM model and integrated 5D BIM workflows for quantity takeoff, cost estimation and scheduling.

REVIT · DYNAMO · BIM · CONSTRUCTION PLANNING
