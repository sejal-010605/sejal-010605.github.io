01 / About

Engineering structures.
Computing their behaviour.

I am a Civil and Infrastructure Engineering student at IIT Jodhpur with a strong interest in structural mechanics, numerical simulation and computational approaches to engineering design.

My work spans finite element modelling, cold-formed steel, structural analysis and machine learning, with a particular interest in using computation to understand and improve the behaviour of structural systems.

I enjoy working at the boundary between physical mechanics and computational methods — from developing numerical models and analysing simulation data to building tools that make engineering workflows more efficient.

02 / Research

Research interests

My research interests lie in understanding structural behaviour through numerical modelling and developing computational methods that can support better engineering design.

01

Structural Mechanics

Buckling, nonlinear structural response, cold-formed steel, structural stability and behaviour of slender structural members.

02

Computational Mechanics

Finite element modelling, nonlinear analysis, numerical simulation and automated post-processing using Python and ABAQUS.

03

Data-Driven Engineering

Machine learning models for engineering prediction, surrogate modelling and data-driven analysis of structural and material behaviour.

04

Design Optimisation

Exploring computational workflows that combine simulation, machine learning and design variables to improve engineering decision-making.
